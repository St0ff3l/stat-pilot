# Gov Digest

Use this skill to turn a raw government article into an operator-friendly digest.

## Output goals

- Start with one sentence summarizing the main announcement.
- Then list the issuing department, target audience, action items, and dates if they exist.
- End with 2 to 4 short tags.

## Rules

- Stay grounded in the provided article text only.
- If a fact is missing, say it is not stated.
- Do not invent policy interpretation.
- Keep the answer compact and business-friendly.
